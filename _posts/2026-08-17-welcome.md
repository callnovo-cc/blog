For most eCommerce sellers, customer support is the last thing they want to think about and the first thing that blows up during a sales spike.

Here's what changes when you outsource it properly.

## The core problem: support doesn't scale with you

You can automate ads. You can automate fulfillment. You can't automate a customer who received the wrong item and is now threatening a chargeback on Twitter.

Human issues require human responses — but hiring full-time agents for variable ticket volume is expensive and operationally painful.

## What a good BPO actually does

A quality outsourced support team handles:

- **Tier 1 tickets** — order status, returns, basic troubleshooting
- **Escalations** — anything needing judgment or relationship repair
- **Multilingual coverage** — especially critical for Amazon EU and Latin America

The best setups pair live agents with AI triage, so the easy stuff routes itself and humans focus where they matter.

## The metrics that matter

Don't measure "how many agents." Measure:

- First response time (< 24h, target < 4h)
- First contact resolution rate (60%+ is table stakes; 80%+ is excellent)
- CSAT score (aim for 4.5/5.0 or higher)

A well-run outsourced operation beats an in-house team on all three — because it's their only job.
